# What Is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating systems that can perform tasks that usually require human intelligence.

## Examples of AI
- ChatGPT: Answers questions and helps with writing.
- Recommendation systems: Suggest movies, music, or videos.
- Voice assistants: Respond to voice commands.

## What I'm Learning
AI systems can process information, recognize patterns, generate content, and help people solve problems. Different AI systems have different capabilities and limitations.

## My Key Takeaway
AI is a technology that can assist humans, but its outputs should be checked for accuracy.
